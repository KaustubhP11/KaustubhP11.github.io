Kaustubh S. Ponkshe

I’m currently a researcher at the famous Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) in Abu Dhabi, where I work with Prof. Praneeth Vepakomma. I graduated from IIT Bombay with a Bachelor’s in EE and a Master’s in AI/ML.

Research

My research focuses on trustworthy, efficient and scalable ML systems. I work on parameter‑efficient fine‑tuning, federated learning and privacy‑preserving AI.
